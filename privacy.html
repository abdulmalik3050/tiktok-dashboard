Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Social Dashboard Sync accesses post performance metrics (views, likes, comments, shares, and related engagement data) from a TikTok account via the official TikTok API, for the sole purpose of displaying this information on a private analytics dashboard.

Data We Access

We access publicly available or account-owned post metrics only. We do not access private messages, personal contact details, or any data unrelated to post performance.

Data Storage

Data retrieved is stored only in a private Google Sheets document controlled by the account owner, and is not shared with, sold to, or accessed by any third party.

Data Retention

Data is retained only as long as needed for reporting purposes and can be deleted at any time by the account owner.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please contact the account owner directly.
